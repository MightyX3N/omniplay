Garry's Mod gamemode · Planned · Name may change

Ride and run together in Garry's Mod.

PhysPlay is a Zwift-style gamemode for GMod. Your real treadmill, bike or footpod moves your character through big worlds, with friends on the same server, and the hills push back on your machine.

Workshop (coming later) About OmniPlay
What it'll be

Your effort, in the game

The plan for the first versions. Details will change as it gets built.

Ride, run or walk

Bikes, treadmills and footpods all work. Your speed in game comes from your real speed or power.

Hills you feel

Climbs slow you down, and on supported bikes the resistance goes up with the gradient.

Multiplayer

Group rides and runs on a server, with everyone's real effort deciding who's in front.

Big routes

Long routes built at GMod's scale, with climbs, sprints and scenery worth the effort.

Segments and leaderboards

Timed climbs and sprints, with server leaderboards to chase.

Live stats

Speed, distance, cadence, power and heart rate on screen as you go.

How it works

OmniPlay does the hardware

01

OmniPlay reads your gear

It talks to your treadmill, bike or footpod over Bluetooth.

02

PhysPlay moves you

The gamemode gets your speed, cadence and power through the OmniPlay mod API.

03

The world pushes back

The gradient under you goes back to OmniPlay, which sets your bike's resistance.
