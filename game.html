Ride the world.

A 3D world you ride, walk or run through on your own bike, treadmill or walking pad. Real places built from OpenStreetMap, hand-made dream worlds, and other players on the road with you. Climbs feel like climbs: the hills go to your machine.

The world

Anywhere, or somewhere new

The game starts from REZVIVO, an open-source indoor-cycling app (GPL-3.0). It already builds terrain, roads, buildings, water and forests from map data as you move, has hand-made Dream Worlds, a rider you can shape and dress in cycling kit, bots to ride with, and workouts.

Real places

Choose a spot on the map and ride, or load a GPX route. The world loads around you.

Dream worlds

Hand-made islands, mountains, canyons and cities that aren't on any map.

Real climbs

The road's gradient becomes resistance on your bike or incline on your treadmill.

What OmniPlay adds

More than a ride

REZVIVO is a training app with a world. OmniPlay turns it into a game.

Every device

Bikes, trainers, sensors, and walking pads and treadmills too, through the OmniPlay SDK. Walk or run the same roads you ride.

Ghosts and segments

Every road is a race against your best and other players' best, with leaderboards per segment.

Live riders nearby

Host with one click over playit.gg. See players riding near you and join, draft or race them.

Exploring

Places to discover, climbs to conquer, collectibles on the road, and a map that fills in as you go.

Unlocks

Earn kits, bikes, colours and dream worlds by riding.

Tour mode

A roguelike run: pick your next stage on a branching map, with elite climbs and rewards. Blow up and the run ends.

Weekly events

One route everyone rides each week, with ghosts, so there's always someone to race.

Asset packs

Swap any texture, model, sound or sky with packs, like resource packs. Stack them in the order you want.

Open source

GPL-3.0, like REZVIVO. Free to play, free to change.

How online works

Players host, a small server connects them

There's no big game server to pay for. Players host rides themselves through playit.gg, and a small OmniPlay server (a Cloudflare Worker) only keeps the list of hosts, the ghosts and the leaderboards.

Host: one click starts a ride and registers it with the server
Player: the game asks the server for hosts riding near you
Riding: you connect straight to the host over playit.gg
After the ride: your ghost and times go to the server
