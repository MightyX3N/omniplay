Your treadmill is the controller.

Walk, run or pedal, and your character moves. OmniPlay connects treadmills, exercise bikes and footpods to any PC game, and lets game mods send hills back to your machine.

Devices

Works with your gear

OmniPlay speaks the open Bluetooth fitness standards, so most current equipment works. Brands with their own protocol get a driver of their own.

Treadmills

FTMS treadmills and Sperax walking pads

Exercise bikes

FTMS bikes, power meters, cadence sensors

Footpods

Zwift RunPod, Stryd, Garmin and other footpods

Heart rate

Any Bluetooth chest strap

Device missing? OmniPlay will include a device explorer that lists what your machine exposes, so support can be added.

For modders

Hills that push back

Connect to a local WebSocket. Read the player's speed, cadence and power, and send terrain back. OmniPlay turns it into bike resistance or treadmill incline, on machines that support it.

Draft. The API isn't built yet and may change.

From OmniPlay, 20× a second
{"t": "state", "speed_kmh": 4.2, "cadence": 108, "power_w": null, "moving": true, "gait": "walk"}
From your game
{"t": "terrain", "grade": 4.5}
