Free · Open source · Early development

Your bike is the controller.

Pedal, walk or run, and the game moves with you. OmniPlay connects exercise bikes, treadmills, walking pads, footpods and heart-rate straps to games, and sends the hills back to your machine.

Read the SDK docs View on GitHub
Two parts

One way in, for any game

The app

Pair your devices in seconds and see them live. Later, a bridge turns your movement into keyboard or gamepad input for other games.

The SDK

Add OmniPlay to your own game. Players install nothing but your game. Lua, JavaScript and Android. Docs

Devices

Works with your gear

OmniPlay speaks the open Bluetooth fitness standards, so most current equipment works. No pairing in Windows settings: turn the device on and pick it from the list.

Exercise bikes

FTMS bikes and smart trainers, with hills as resistance

Treadmills

FTMS treadmills and Sperax walking pads, with speed control

Sensors

Speed and cadence sensors, footpods

Heart rate

Any Bluetooth chest strap
